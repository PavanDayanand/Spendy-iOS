This index covers 158 topic pages under the six HIG sections and the eight Components groups. Each comma-separated item after a page is an in-page section ID: append `#ID` to that page's URL. Read the live page for its actual guidance, tables, imagery, and examples. Where present, pages also contain Resources (Related, Developer documentation, Videos) and Change log sections; follow those links as needed. The index omits repeated resource headings, not their contents. A future Apple update may add or rename topics or anchors. A cross-link audit of the 158 topic articles found no additional destination topics. Four older links redirect to indexed pages: `spatial-interactions` to Nearby interactions, and nested component URLs for Complications, Page controls, and Tab views to their current flat URLs.
