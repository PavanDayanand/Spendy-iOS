# Design sources

These are retained source artwork and editable icon materials for Spendy. The original PNG pixels are preserved.

## Main mascot variants

- `SourceArtwork/Mascots/mascot-coffee-original.png` is the original c8 coffee mascot used by the active app icon.
- `SourceArtwork/Mascots/mascot-fire.png` is the c4 fire variant.
- `SourceArtwork/Mascots/mascot-cash.png` is the c7 cash variant.
- The remaining numbered variants retain their original c1, c2, c3, c5, and c6 sequence numbers.

## Editable icon source

`SourceArtwork/IconComposer/Spendy.icon` is the editable final blue design. It contains only the original mascot, with no surrounding objects or glass backdrop. Original reference artwork remains in `Assets/reference-layer.png`. `Spendy-Colorways.json` stores the three native light/dark background palettes.

The app includes `Spendy/SpendyFrosted.icon`, `Spendy/SpendyFrostedEmber.icon`, and `Spendy/SpendyFrostedGraphite.icon`. Appearance offers three colorways and an original-icon reset. `AppIconPreviews/icon-colorways.jpg` shows the final six appearances; runtime picker thumbnails live in their asset-catalog image sets.

The active Xcode icon is `Spendy/Spendy_iOS.icon`. Rive animation assets live in `Spendy/Resources/`, and the runtime Insights artwork lives in `Spendy/Assets.xcassets/SpendyInsightsLogo.imageset/`.
